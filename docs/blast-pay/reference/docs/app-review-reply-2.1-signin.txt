Hello,

Thank you for the detail — it let us find the cause. We have fixed it in
build 1.0 (6), which is attached to this submission.

WHAT WENT WRONG

The credentials were valid, but the password field was not on the first
screen. Signing in with a password was behind a secondary text link
("Sign in with a password") below the main button. Tapping the main
button sent a one-time link to review@blastpay.tj — a mailbox the
reviewer has no access to — which is a dead end. We can see in our logs
that the session then continued through Sign in with Apple instead.

WHAT WE CHANGED

1. The secondary link is gone. There is now one button. After entering
   the email address and tapping it, the app asks the server how that
   address signs in and shows the password field directly for accounts
   that have one. For the demo account this means:

       enter review@blastpay.tj  →  tap the button  →  password field
       enter reviewApple1        →  signed in

   No hidden step, nothing to discover.

2. Early access is switched off for the review period. Previously new
   accounts joined a waiting queue before reaching the catalogue. Any
   account now has full access immediately — including the Sign in with
   Apple account created during your review, which we have granted
   access to as well. The reviewer may use Sign in with Apple, Google,
   an email link, or the demo credentials; all four give the same full
   functionality.

3. The "Terms" and "Privacy Policy" links on the sign-in screen were
   coloured as links but did not respond to taps. They now open
   https://www.blastpay.tj/terms and https://www.blastpay.tj/privacy
   (English versions at /en/terms and /en/privacy).

DEMO ACCOUNT (unchanged)

    Email:    review@blastpay.tj
    Password: <ВЫРЕЗАНО ПРИ УПАКОВКЕ — см. App Store Connect → Sign-In Information>

The account has location and QR checks disabled, so a discount can be
activated from anywhere in the world and the complete flow — catalogue,
venue, offer conditions, activation, bill entry, visit history, review
with photo, account deletion — is testable outside Tajikistan.

The only step that cannot complete outside Tajikistan is the hand-off to
local bank applications (Alif, Eskhata, Amonatbonk), which are not
distributed elsewhere. No payment is charged during review, and the
discount is applied by the venue regardless of payment method.

Thank you,
Blast Pay team
