Hello,

Two statements in our previous message were inaccurate. We are correcting
them ourselves rather than letting the reviewer discover the discrepancy.

1. CORRECTION — THE APP DOES CONTAIN USER-GENERATED CONTENT

We wrote that it does not. That is wrong, and we apologise for the
confusion. After a visit, a guest can leave a star rating, a written review
and photos, and these are shown to other guests on the venue page. The app
has no chat and no user-to-user messaging.

The mechanisms required by Guideline 1.2 are present in the build already
submitted (1.0.2, build 5):

- Only a guest whose visit the app itself verified can write a review, and
  only one review per visit. Reviews cannot be posted anonymously, in bulk,
  or without having been to the venue.
- Every review written by another guest has a Report action with four
  reasons: spam, offensive content, fake review, other.
- Three reports hide a review immediately, pending a moderator decision.
- Our published rules prohibit offensive content, other people's personal
  data and advertising. We remove content that breaks them.
- Contact for content concerns: support@blastpay.tj

How to reach it with the demo account: open any venue from the main list,
scroll to the reviews section, tap the "..." control on a review left by
another guest, and choose Report.

2. CORRECTION — MAPS

We listed "Google Maps / MapKit". The app does not use Google Maps. On iOS
the map is Apple MapKit. OpenStreetMap tiles are used only in our Android
build, which is not part of this submission.

The corrected list of external services for point 4 is:
  - Supabase — database, authentication and file storage
  - Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, magic email links — authentication
  - Apple MapKit — maps and venue locations
  - SmartPay — payment gateway that hands off to local bank applications
No SMS providers, no AI services, no advertising networks, no third-party
tracking SDKs.
